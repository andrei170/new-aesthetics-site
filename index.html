About the Practice

A practicing DO who owns the room

Dr. Jason Schave grew up in Marshfield, Wisconsin and completed his training as a Doctor of Osteopathic Medicine, with a specialty in Physical Medicine and Rehabilitation. He has practiced clinical medicine for more than 24 years across Wisconsin, and founded NEW Aesthetics on Main Street in Green Bay to bring that same physician-first mindset to aesthetic care.

The difference at NEW Aesthetics is straightforward: the owner is the doctor, and the doctor is present. The philosophy Dr. Schave built the clinic on is delivering high-quality aesthetic results without inflated pricing and without steering patients toward services they do not need. That commitment is possible because the practice is not owned by investors or a management group. It is owned by the clinician who sees you.

Frequently asked

Questions patients ask before their first visit

Straightforward answers about what makes NEW Aesthetics different and what to expect when you come in.

Is NEW Aesthetics run by a physician? +

Yes. NEW Aesthetics is owned and operated by Dr. Jason Schave, a Doctor of Osteopathic Medicine whose specialty is Physical Medicine and Rehabilitation. A DO holds the same full physician license as an MD in Wisconsin. Dr. Schave is not a contracted medical director. He is the clinic owner and is present in the practice.

What is a DO, and why does it matter for aesthetics? +

A Doctor of Osteopathic Medicine completes the same medical school curriculum as an MD, plus additional training in the musculoskeletal system and how the body functions as a whole. Both degrees carry identical scope of practice and prescribing authority in Wisconsin. For aesthetic patients, the practical difference is that Dr. Schave reads your face and body through a clinician's lens, not a sales lens.

Will I be pressured to buy packages or extra services? +

No. The clinic's stated philosophy is delivering high-quality aesthetic care without inflated pricing and without pressure to purchase services you did not ask for. Because the owner is the physician, that commitment holds from the top of the practice down.

Where is the clinic located and how do I get there? +

NEW Aesthetics is at 1270 Main Street, Suite LL4, Green Bay, Wisconsin 54302. The clinic is in a lower-level suite of a Main Street building in the heart of Green Bay. Street parking is available along Main Street. You can also find the clinic on Instagram at @newaestheticsgb for updates and photos.

What should I do before my first appointment? +

Book a consultation by calling or texting the clinic directly. At your first visit, the team will walk you through the services that fit your goals and give you a clear picture of what to expect in terms of results, timing, and pricing. No commitment is required at the consultation stage.
